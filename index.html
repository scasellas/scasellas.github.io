AI automation for a personal operating system

I designed and run an operating layer that makes calls for me, books appointments, keeps my calendar, reads my running and health logs and surfaces what matters on its own, with clear limits where human judgment or approval matters.

  • Makes calls for meA bilingual voice agent that says it's an assistant, keeps transcripts and recordings, and books within the limits I set.
  • Books appointmentsAnd puts them on my calendar, then checks the entry is really there.
  • Turns email into eventsOnly when the date is in the future and the time has an explicit AM or PM. Anything unclear is flagged.
  • Reads my running and health logsFolds them into scheduled reviews, next to household admin, planning and open opportunities.
  • Surfaces things on its ownReminders, reviews and changes worth knowing about, without being asked, and silence when there's nothing new.
  • Keeps knowledge searchableResearch, notes, plans and meetings in one base, raw capture kept apart from reviewed guidance.

The challenge

Personal and small-business work was spread across email, calendars, documents, spreadsheets, messaging, research and manual follow-ups.

Automation had to work across those boundaries without creating duplicate events, inventing missing details, exposing private information or taking consequential actions without permission.

Safety and privacy by design

  • Human approval for spending, public publishing, credential handling, destructive changes, sensitive security decisions and any commitment not explicitly authorized.
  • No inventing facts: when evidence is incomplete, it asks for review or reports the limitation.
  • AI-generated actions are checked against the source or destination system wherever practical.
  • Names, contact details, credentials and private infrastructure are left out of this case study.

Email to calendar

Scans recent email for event messages and creates an entry only when a future date and an explicit AM/PM time are unambiguous. Unclear messages are flagged, not guessed. Tested; the latest run completed with no events to add.

Recurring Life OS automations

Scheduled reviews and reminders for household admin, health and running data, planning and open opportunities. Script-backed jobs stay silent when there is nothing useful to report and surface errors when action is needed.

Knowledge and planning

Research, notes, plans and meeting material in a searchable knowledge base, with raw capture kept apart from reviewed, reusable guidance.

Bilingual call workflow

An AI-assisted phone workflow with a transparent assistant identity, transcripts and recordings for diagnosis, and booking guardrails. Payment, deposits, changed terms or an unapproved commitment stop for human direction.

Business-development support

Research, lead lists, outreach scripts and pipeline tracking for a services initiative. First contact stays founder-led, not delegated to an AI voice agent.

Reliability monitoring

Scheduled health checks, alert suppression for unchanged conditions, backups and operational reviews. Fixed scheduled jobs pinned to a stale model configuration and verified successful runs where safe.

Results

Working integrations, scheduled automations, monitoring scripts and reusable operating procedures, not a prototype or a prompt collection. Several workflows have run against live systems, including calendar sync and scheduled reports.

Operation exposed real problems, and they were fixed: stale model pins, unsafe date inference, misleading job-status reporting and repeated alerts for unchanged conditions.

Scope and status

An evolving, personally operated system. Not a claim of universal reliability, quantified time savings or a fully autonomous agent. Results depend on source-system access, provider availability and ongoing monitoring.

The design favours practical integration, conservative action boundaries and observable behaviour over autonomy for its own sake.
